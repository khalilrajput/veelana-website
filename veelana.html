EASY APPLICATION ROUTINE

How to Use for Optimal Hair Growth

Follow this simple 3-step routine 3 times every week for noticeable results in 14 days.

1

Warm & Dispense

Pour 4–6ml of Veelana oil into your palm. Rub palms together to gently warm the botanical blend.

2

Scalp Massage (5 mins)

Massage deeply into the scalp using circular fingertip motions. Focus on thinning areas and crown.

3

Leave & Rinse

Leave for at least 2 hours or overnight. Wash with a mild sulphate-free shampoo. Repeat 3x weekly.
