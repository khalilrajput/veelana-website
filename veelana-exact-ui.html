The Veelana 4-Step Ritual

How to Use for Maximum Hair Growth

Follow this simple ritual 3 times a week to stimulate dormant follicles, reduce daily shedding, and lock in healthy gloss.

01Apply

Targeted Scalp Drops

Section your hair into parts and apply 3–5 drops of Veelana Oil directly to scalp using the precision dropper.

Tip: Focus on hairline & thinning areas
02Massage

5–10 Mins Circulation

Gently massage roots in slow circular motions using fingertips to stimulate blood flow and follicle oxygenation.

Tip: Warm oil in hands for faster absorption
03Nourish

2 Hours or Overnight

Allow the 26 raw botanical herbs to deeply penetrate the scalp pores. Leave overnight or minimum 2 hours.

Tip: Wrap in a silk cap or towel overnight
04Rinse

Gentle Herbal Wash

Wash off thoroughly with a mild sulphate-free shampoo to reveal silky, hydrated, non-greasy natural volume.

Tip: Repeat 3x weekly for visible regrowth
Routine: 3 Times a Week
Duration: 2 Hours / Overnight
